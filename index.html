Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Mihir R. Shah

Dr. Mihir R. Shah is an experienced Dentist and Implantologist practicing in Nanpura, Surat.

He has over 23 years of clinical experience and completed his BDS from Government Dental College, Jamnagar in 2001.

He specializes in dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, crowns, bridges, dentures, root canal treatments, braces, and preventive dental care.

He is committed to providing painless, ethical, and patient-focused dental treatment using modern technology and international standards.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

No. With modern anaesthesia and techniques, root canal treatment at Smile Care is virtually painless and completed comfortably.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular checkups, dental implants can last 15-25 years or even a lifetime in many cases.

Do you treat children?

Yes, we offer gentle pediatric dentistry designed to make children feel comfortable and build positive dental habits early.

Do you accept dental emergencies?

Yes, we provide prompt emergency dental care for pain, trauma and accidents — just call our clinic directly.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a routine dental checkup and cleaning every six months to catch issues early and maintain oral health.
